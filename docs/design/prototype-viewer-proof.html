The Lantern and the Long Night

Beat four · virtue tested — courage

The wind leaned hard on Bramblewood that night, and one by one the hedgerow lamps went out — all but the old cedar lantern in Pip’s paws. The mill path lay black as ink, and the dark was the one thing Pip had never been brave about.

“Small light, small steps,” he whispered, the way Papa said it. One paw, then another. The lantern reached only to the next step — but it always reached that far. And that was enough.
